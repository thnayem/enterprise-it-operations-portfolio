# IT Management & Infrastructure Operations

This section documents the management work that keeps IT services dependable: planning capacity, maintaining an accurate inventory, coordinating suppliers, controlling procurement, preparing for service disruption, and supporting a team through clear priorities and follow-up.

The material is written as practical working references. It describes processes and templates, not claims of a particular budget, team size, procurement authority, data-centre ownership, or recovery result.

## Management areas

- [Capacity planning](capacity-planning.md) — demand, utilization, headroom, lifecycle and upgrade decisions.
- [Data centre and disaster recovery](data-centre-disaster-recovery.md) — facility controls, dependencies, RPO/RTO, backup and recovery exercises.
- [Power, UPS, solar, cooling and rack planning](power-cooling-rack-planning.md) — online UPS sizing, backup runtime, generator, energy consumption, solar PV, cooling capacity, airflow, rack layout and maintenance.
- [Vendor and service management](vendor-management.md) — supplier evaluation, SLA tracking, escalation and renewal planning.
- [IT asset and inventory management](asset-inventory-management.md) — asset lifecycle, ownership, stock control, audit and reconciliation.
- [IT procurement](it-procurement.md) — requirements, specifications, quotations, evaluation, approval and acceptance.
- [Printer toner and consumables](toner-consumables-management.md) — usage tracking, reorder points, stock rotation and cost control.
- [Team leadership and service operations](team-leadership-service-operations.md) — work allocation, escalation, handover, service reviews and operational metrics.

## How to use these references

Adapt the templates to the organization's approval matrix, finance and procurement policies, security requirements, and actual service commitments. Keep the live asset register, quotations, contracts, credentials, site details, serial numbers and financial information in approved internal systems, not in this public repository.
